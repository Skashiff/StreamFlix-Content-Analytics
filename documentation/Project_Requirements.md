# StreamFlix Content Analytics — Project Requirements

## Business objective

Analyse streaming-platform data to understand subscriber behaviour, content performance, engagement, churn, and catalogue investment, then translate the findings into leadership-ready KPIs, an interactive Power BI dashboard, and a management summary.

## Scope

- Viewing history: 2017 through May 2026
- Subscriber status reference date: 1 June 2026
- 20 countries
- 16 primary genres
- 6 device types
- 3 subscription plans: Basic with Ads, Standard, Premium

## Phase 1 — Data Cleaning & Quality

- Load and profile all six datasets.
- Check missing values, duplicates and data types.
- Validate subscriber churn rules.
- Validate viewing duration and completion percentages.
- Check review sentiment categories.
- Verify referential integrity between fact/event tables and subscriber/title dimensions.
- Export cleaned datasets and document data-quality findings.

## Phase 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis

Required visuals:

1. Monthly viewing volume
2. Monthly watch-hours trend
3. Genre-wise watch-hours share
4. Movies vs TV Shows split
5. Top 10 subscriber countries by watch hours
6. Subscriber plan distribution
7. Device usage
8. Subscriber age distribution
9. Completion rate by genre
10. Review sentiment breakdown

## Phase 3 — KPI Calculations

Required KPIs:

- Total Watch Hours
- Active Rate
- Churn Rate
- Average Completion Rate
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- ARPU
- Average Watch Time per Subscriber
- Watchlist Conversion
- Hit Concentration
- Originals Share of Hours

Bonus: signup-cohort retention at 3, 6 and 12 months.

## Phase 4 — Dashboard & Management Report

Power BI dashboard pages:

1. Engagement Overview
2. Content Performance
3. Subscriber Insights
4. Experience
5. Catalogue & Investment

Each page includes a title, multiple visuals, slicers/filters, and key management insights.

The final management report is addressed to the CEO and covers platform health, top findings, risks, opportunities and actionable recommendations.
